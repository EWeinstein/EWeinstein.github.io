ML and Molecules Reading Group — Fall 2026

The ML & Molecules reading group covers fundamental machine learning methodology and its intersection with the molecular sciences. This semester we will study reinforcement learning in the laboratory. We will examine general frameworks for sequential decision making, and learn about the connections to probabilistic inference. We will examine methods for optimizing reward models, and their application to the design of molecules. We will think about the unique challenges associated with applying these techniques to control iterative laboratory experimentation.

About ML & Molecules: ML & Molecules is a reading group organized by Asst. Prof. Eli Weinstein (DTU Chemistry) and Assoc. Prof. Jes Frellsen (DTU Compute). It focuses on fundamental probabilistic machine learning and its intersection with the molecular sciences. Everyone in the group presents a paper, in a rotating schedule. It is open to students, postdocs, faculty and staff. It is not a formal course, but can optionally be taken as a project course for course credit. Contact: enawe [at] dtu.dk
